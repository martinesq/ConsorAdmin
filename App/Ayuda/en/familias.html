Families

What is it for?

A family is a group of patents (for example Admin or Condominium manager). It is built once and then assigned to users.

Steps

  1. Go to Families.
  2. To create one press New Family, type the name and press Create.
  3. Choose the family in the list.
  4. Choose a patent or a family and press <--Add. To remove one press Remove-->.
  5. Press Save.

Common errors

  • If you see ya existe esta patente en esta familia: the patent is already in the family, directly or inside another family it contains.
  • The family is not created: the name is missing before pressing Create.
