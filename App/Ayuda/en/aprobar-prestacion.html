Approve service

What is it for?

The loaded work is reviewed and approved or rejected. If it is approved it becomes Aprobado and later it is paid from Register supplier payment.

Steps

  1. Go to Service Management > Approve service.
  2. Choose the condominium and the service. The detail is shown on the right.
  3. To approve press Approve service.
  4. To reject type the reason and press Reject service. It becomes Rechazado.

Common errors

  • If you see No hay prestaciones pendientes de aprobacion: there is no work to review in that condominium.
  • If you see You must enter a reason to reject: type the reason before rejecting.
