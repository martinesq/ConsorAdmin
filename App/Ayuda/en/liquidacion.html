Generate maintenance fee settlement

What is it for?

Splits the costs of a period among the active units according to each unit coefficient and generates one fee per unit.

Steps

  1. Go to Fee Settlement > Generate maintenance fee settlement.
  2. Choose the condominium and the period. Only periods with unsettled costs are listed.
  3. Check the total costs, choose the due date and enter the management fee %.
  4. Press Generate settlement. The grid shows the fee of each unit.

Common errors

  • If you see No hay períodos con gastos pendientes de liquidar: there are no periods to settle, load the costs first.
  • If you see El porcentaje de honorario debe estar entre 0% y 100%: fix the percentage.
  • If you see La fecha de vencimiento no puede ser anterior a la fecha actual: choose a date from today on.
  • If you see La fecha de vencimiento no puede ser anterior al ...: the due date must be after the end of the period and not before the one of settled periods. It is better to keep the date the screen suggests.
