Register supplier payment

What is it for?

Registers a payment that leaves the condominium fund, either an approved service or a supply purchase. The payment is loaded as a cost for the next settlement.

Steps

  1. Go to Fee Settlement > Register supplier payment.
  2. Choose the condominium. The available balance and the approved services still unpaid are shown.
  3. To pay a service choose it in the grid. For a supply purchase do not choose any service and fill in concept and amount.
  4. Press Confirm payment.

Common errors

  • If you see El consorcio no tiene servicios aprobados pendientes de pago: only a supply purchase can be loaded.
  • If you see insufficient balance for this payment: the amount is higher than the available balance, the button stays disabled until it is fixed.
  • If you see Saldo insuficiente: the balance changed before confirming, check the available balance.
  • The Confirm payment button is disabled: choose a service or fill in concept and amount.
