Collect maintenance fee

What is it for?

Registers the full or partial payment of a unit fee and generates the receipt.

Steps

  1. Go to Fee Settlement > Collect maintenance fee.
  2. Choose the condominium and the unit. Its pending fees are listed.
  3. Choose the fee. The amount, paid amount, balance, due date and status are shown above.
  4. Enter the amount, choose the payment method and press Collect maintenance fee.
  5. If everything is paid the fee becomes Pagada and the receipt is shown. If only a part is paid the remaining balance is shown.

Common errors

  • If you see La unidad no tiene expensas pendientes de pago: the unit has no pending fees.
  • If you see El monto ingresado supera la deuda. ¿Confirma el excedente ... como saldo a favor?: the amount is higher than the debt. Press Yes to register it anyway or No to fix the amount.
