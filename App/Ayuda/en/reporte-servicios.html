Services report

What is it for?

Lists the services of a condominium or a supplier with their status and the accumulated cost. It can be exported to PDF.

Steps

  1. Go to Service Management > Services report.
  2. Choose By consortium or By supplier and then which one.
  3. Press Generate.
  4. Press Export PDF to save it.

Common errors

  • If you see Select a consortium / Select a supplier: choose one before generating.
  • If you see The PDF could not be generated, please try again: export again.
