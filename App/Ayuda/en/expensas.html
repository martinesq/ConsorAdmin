Cancel maintenance fee

What is it for?

Used to cancel a pending or overdue fee that was settled wrong. The reason has to be written down.

Steps

  1. Go to Fee Settlement > Cancel maintenance fee.
  2. Choose the condominium. Its pending or overdue fees are listed.
  3. Choose the fee and type the reason.
  4. Press Void.

Common errors

  • If you see El consorcio no tiene expensas pendientes o vencidas: there is nothing to cancel in that condominium.
  • The Void button is disabled: the fee or the reason is missing.
  • If you see No se puede anular una expensa con pagos imputados: a fee that already has payments cannot be cancelled.
