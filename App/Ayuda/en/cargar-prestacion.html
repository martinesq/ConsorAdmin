Record supplier service

What is it for?

When the supplier finishes the work, what was done, the cost and the invoice are loaded. The service becomes Prestado.

Steps

  1. Go to Service Management > Record supplier service.
  2. Choose the condominium and the service.
  3. Fill in date, work performed, cost and invoice number. The file is optional and is added with Attach...
  4. Press Confirm entry.

Common errors

  • If you see No hay servicios asignados pendientes de carga: there are no assigned services in that condominium.
  • The Confirm entry button is disabled: cost or invoice number is missing.
  • If you see El costo debe ser mayor a cero: enter the cost of the work.
