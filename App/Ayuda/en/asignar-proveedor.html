Assign supplier to service

What is it for?

Chooses which supplier does the work of a request and in how many days. The service becomes Asignado.

Steps

  1. Go to Service Management > Assign supplier to service.
  2. Choose the condominium. The requests pending assignment are listed.
  3. Choose the request, the supplier and the estimated term in days.
  4. Press Confirm assignment.

Common errors

  • If you see No hay solicitudes pendientes de asignacion: there are no requests for that condominium.
  • If you see No hay proveedores habilitados: load or enable a supplier in Condominium maintenance.
  • If you see You must enter a valid estimated term: the term has to be greater than zero.
