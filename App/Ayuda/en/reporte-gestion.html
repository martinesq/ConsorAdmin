Management report

What is it for?

Puts together the composition of a fee, the delinquency, the fund status and the account statement of a condominium. It can be exported to PDF.

Steps

  1. Go to Fee Settlement > Management report.
  2. Choose the condominium and the filter of each tab (unit, period or unit / whole condominium).
  3. Press Generate.
  4. In Review breakdown choose a fee to see its costs. In Delinquency statistics choose a unit to see the detail.
  5. Press Export PDF to save the four queries in one file.

Common errors

  • If you see You have no assigned consortiums: the user does not manage any condominium.
  • If you see El consorcio no registra unidades morosas / El período no registra pagos ni egresos / No hay movimientos registrados: it is not an error, there is no data for that filter.
  • If you see No se pudo generar el PDF, intente nuevamente: the PDF could not be generated, export again.
