Generate payment order

What is it for?

Generates the payment order (stub) of a unit settled fee, with the owner, the amount and the due date.

Steps

  1. Go to Fee Settlement > Generate payment order.
  2. Choose the condominium. The units with a settled fee and no order yet are listed.
  3. Choose the unit and press Generate payment order.
  4. The payment order stub shows the order number, owner, amount and due date.

Common errors

  • If you see No hay unidades con expensa liquidada pendiente de orden de pago: all units already have their order or the settlement is missing.
  • The Generate payment order button is disabled: choose a unit in the grid.
